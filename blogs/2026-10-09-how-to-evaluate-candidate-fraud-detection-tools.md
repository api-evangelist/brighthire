TL;DR: Key considerations when evaluating candidate fraud detection vendors include false positive rates, ATS integration depth, verifiable hiring records, candidate experience impact, interviewer adoption, and the range of fraud signals the tool can detect. Detection that runs during the interview can reduce adoption friction and create a more complete hiring record than standalone verification steps...
