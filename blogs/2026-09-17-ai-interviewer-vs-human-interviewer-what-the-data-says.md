TL;DR: AI and human interviewers are strongest at different parts of the process. AI gathers consistent signal at volume, asking every candidate the same questions, scoring them against the same rubric, and documenting the answers the same way every time. Humans review that signal, decide who advances, and run the interviews that follow.
